The I.A.N.s

Their objective was to facilitate the retrieval of these high-valued assets without the need of direct contact of our human resources and minimizing possible oversights, discrepancies or frictions due to human interaction.
The I.A.N units were created with a complex AI that has a clear directive, yet yields the capability to self-manage and overcome all obstacles possible with precise computational accuracy and calculations to insure the most profitable and least adverse outcomes. These units were extensively trained and tested to ensure this AI is ready to adapt to all possibilities in the Oberon moon, one of the 29 moons of Uranus.

Our Mission? Simple. Save Humanity

X-corps was founded in 2032 after NASA, ESA and Roscosmos fell in a very negative light subsequent to the Perseverance Mission and related material retrieval initiatives from Mars did not achieve their intended outcomes. Despite an aggregate investment exceeding 400 billion in capital allocation and the regrettable loss of two human resources during a manned exploration and retrieval endeavor, these efforts did not yield the anticipated operational synergies.
Thus X-corps was founded. The company's mission was simple.
Leveraging strategic partnerships and optimized resource allocation and access.

And lastly, this one. Our success

Following the successful global ratification of Proposition 43 in 2068, we achieved full stakeholder alignment and secured the necessary permissions to leverage our strategic resources. This milestone enables the deployment of our I.A.N. units alongside a select cohort of high-value human assets to the meteor's point of origin, in pursuit of optimizing asset retrieval and furthering our long-term operational objectives.
Ensuring the future is ours.
